More Than a Personality Type

You may have heard of the MBTI personality test. Perhaps you have taken a personality test, discussed your results with friends, or come across familiar combinations of letters such as INFP, ENTP, and ISTP.

But have you wondered what lies behind those four letters? The Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI) was inspired by the theory of psychological types developed by Swiss psychiatrist Carl Gustav Jung. His theory explores how people perceive the world, process information and make decisions.

Rather than simply describing what a person is like, cognitive functions offer a framework for exploring people's inner thinking&feeling style and how they know the world.

As someone who identifies with ISTP, I am interested in the different ways people experience and interpret the same world.

I created this website to introduce some cognitive functions which explore their possible connections with creativity, and at the end I will share some resources for those who wish to learn more.
