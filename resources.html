A Place to Start

Understanding yourself often begins with curiosity. Explore Your Cognitive Preferences Curious about your own cognitive functions? These websites offer different ways to explore personality theory and reflect on your thinking preferences.

01 — Sakinorva

A detailed cognitive function test that gives you individual function scores and compares your results across several typing models. A good choice if you'd like to explore your cognitive preferences in more depth.
  • Sakinorva Cognitive Functions Test

    • 02 — Michael Caloz

    A beginner-friendly test that explains cognitive functions through everyday situations and practical examples. A useful place to start if you're still getting familiar with the theory.
  • Michael Caloz Cognitive Functions Test

    • 03 — JUNGUS

    A Chinese-language platform that takes a different approach to Jungian-inspired personality theory, using its own model of sixteen cognitive functions and sixty-four personality types. Worth exploring if you're interested in alternative interpretations of personality theory.
  • JUNGUS Cognitive Functions Test

    • Before You Take a Test

    You might notice that different tests give you different results. That's not unusual. Each test has its own way of asking questions and interpreting your answers. It's worth remembering that these tests aren't psychological diagnoses, nor can they tell you everything about who you are. Think of them as starting points for self-reflection. You might recognize yourself in some descriptions and question others. Both can be part of learning more about yourself.

    A personality type can help you describe who you are, but it shouldn't decide who you have to become.
